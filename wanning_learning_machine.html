How to explore. Press Start, and from that moment it is alive: it studies your cards over and over on its own, synapses strengthen and weaken, weak unused ones are pruned and regrown elsewhere, and the brain map changes in front of you. The unseen-pairs meter reads its understanding as it develops - the preloaded cards carry the paper's rule (every symbol shifted up by two) with four pairs held back that it never sees in a lesson, so a rising meter means the rule itself is taking shape inside it; full mastery takes a long development, as it does in the paper. Ask anything at any moment, mid-life: a question reads its mind through a frozen probe and leaves it exactly as it was. Pause holds it perfectly still - nothing moves until you start it again. Reset hands you a newborn brain. Replace the cards with any pattern of your own - a classification with several inputs to one label, or any mapping - and raise it on that instead.
